About Blood Donation

Blood donation is a voluntary process in which a healthy person donates blood to help patients who need it. Donated blood can help people during surgeries, accidents, childbirth complications and serious illnesses.

Why Is Blood Donation Important?

Blood cannot be manufactured artificially. Hospitals need a regular supply of donated blood to treat patients. A single donation may help more than one patient when blood components are separated.

  • Helps accident and emergency patients.
  • Supports patients undergoing major surgery.
  • Helps people with severe anaemia and blood disorders.
  • Supports mothers with childbirth complications.

Our Message

Who Can Donate?

Many healthy adults can donate blood, but eligibility depends on health checks and local blood donation rules.

  • Be in good general health.
  • Meet the required age and weight criteria.
  • Pass the health screening at the blood centre.
  • Follow the advice of medical professionals.

Did You Know?

Donated blood can be separated into red blood cells, platelets and plasma for different medical needs.
