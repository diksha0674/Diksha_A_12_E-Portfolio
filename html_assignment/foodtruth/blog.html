DAIRY PRODUCTS

Milk Adulteration: What Should You Know?

Milk is an important part of our daily diet. However, milk adulteration can affect its quality. Water dilution and unauthorized additives are among the concerns consumers should know about.

How to Stay Safe?

  • Purchase milk from reliable sources.
  • Check packaging and expiry information.
  • Prefer properly labelled dairy products.
  • Report suspicious products to food authorities.

BEVERAGES

Are Your Favourite Drinks Safe?

Packaged juices and other beverages should follow food safety standards. Unauthorized colours, misleading labels and poor storage can affect product safety.

Consumer Safety Tips

  • Read the ingredient list carefully.
  • Check the manufacturing and expiry dates.
  • Avoid damaged or leaking packages.
  • Check the product's food licence details.
