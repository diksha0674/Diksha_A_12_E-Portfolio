Food Categories

Explore different types of food adulteration concerns.

🥛 Milk and Dairy Products

Milk, cheese, butter and other dairy products require proper quality checks. Water dilution and unauthorized substances are common concerns.

🌶️ Spices and Pulses

Spices and pulses may be mixed with inferior materials or unauthorized colours, affecting their purity and quality.

🧃 Food and Beverages

Fruit juices, soft drinks and other beverages should meet food safety standards and contain only permitted ingredients.

🍎 Fruits and Vegetables

Improper use of chemicals, artificial colours and pesticide residues can raise food safety concerns.

🍯 Oils and Sweeteners

Cooking oils, honey and other sweeteners should be checked for purity and proper labelling.
