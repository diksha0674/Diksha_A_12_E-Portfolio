About FoodTruth

Creating awareness about food quality and safety.

Our Mission

FoodTruth is an awareness platform designed to educate people about adulteration in food and beverages. Our mission is to encourage informed decisions and safer food habits.

Our Objectives

  • Spread awareness about food adulteration.
  • Educate consumers about food safety.
  • Promote responsible food purchasing habits.
  • Share useful food quality information.
